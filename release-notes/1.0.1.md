**Procedure boxes, easier to write**
- Edit a procedure right where it sits in your note: the steps on top, the typeset result live underneath as you type. Click away or press ⌘↵ to keep it, Esc to undo your changes.
- Less LaTeX typing: subscripts and powers like `x_i`, `p_{LM}`, `x^2`, `10^9` and `log_2 n` are typeset without dollar signs, just like `\in` and `\Sigma` already were. Ordinary words such as `max_len` stay as written.
- A new procedure starts blank, and long steps wrap instead of running off the edge.
