The first release of Notival you can download and keep up to date.

**Install**
1. Download **Notival.zip** below and open it.
2. Drag **Notival** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the developer: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Notival. You only do this once.

From then on Notival keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out it offers it when you open the app, and installs it in a click. Your notes are kept separately and are never touched by an update.

**What's in it**
- Notes for every subject, with Recall: questions you write (or keep from suggestions) come back for review when you're about to forget them.
- Class mode: set the class length, go full screen, a countdown with live stats, and a quick wrap-up afterwards.
- Class schedule: enter your timetable once and Notival offers to start each class in today's note.
- Procedure boxes for pseudocode and step-by-step methods, typeset like a textbook; LaTeX works inside them.
- Equations, columns, pictures beside text, mind maps, PDF notes and export to PDF or Word.
